About this atta

Whole grain, nothing taken out.

Our Sharbati Whole Wheat Atta is made from a single grain: Sharbati wheat from our partner farms, with protein of 12% or more and moisture held at 11–12%. Every lot is hand-sorted twice, rested, then ground slowly between granite stones. The mill never rises above 40 °C, so the germ oils stay intact and the flour keeps its sweet, toasty aroma.

We pack it the same day in breathable paper-lined bags, and it reaches your door within 24 hours of milling.

What makes it different

  • Stone-ground, not roller-milled. Slow granite stones crush the whole kernel instead of separating and recombining it.
  • Bran and germ included. More fibre, B vitamins and natural oils than refined or high-speed milled flour.
  • Milled in small batches. We grind to order, so there is no warehouse stock sitting for months.
  • Naturally sweet Sharbati wheat. A golden grain known for soft dough and rotis that puff.
  • Nothing added. No improvers, bleach, preservatives or anti-caking agents.

How to use

Rotis, parathas and bread.

Fresh whole wheat drinks more water than store-bought atta, so start with the ratios below and adjust by a spoonful.

Soft rotis

  • 250 g atta (about 2 cups)
  • 130–140 ml warm water (about 55%)
  • 1/4 tsp salt, 1 tsp oil
  • Knead 8 minutes, rest 20 minutes

Layered parathas

  • 250 g atta
  • 150 ml water (about 60%)
  • 1 tbsp ghee in the dough, 1 tsp per fold
  • Rest 30 minutes, roll thin, cook on a hot tawa

Whole wheat bread

  • 500 g atta (or 350 g atta + 150 g bread flour)
  • 350 ml water (about 70%)
  • 7 g instant yeast, 10 g salt, 15 g oil
  • Prove 1 hour, shape, prove 45 minutes, bake 35 minutes at 200 °C
Storage & shelf life

Best within 3 months.

Because the germ oils are still in the flour, whole wheat atta is best used within 3 months of milling. Keep it cool, dry and sealed.

  • Decant into an airtight steel or glass container after opening.
  • Keep it away from the stove, sun and damp.
  • In humid weather or hot kitchens, store it in the fridge.
  • Your pack shows the milling date and a best-before date.
