About Millstone & Co.

A family flour mill, still turning the same granite.

Since 1987 the Becker family has milled whole grain the slow way, then delivered it while it is still warm from the stones.

Our story

From one garage chakki to 3,200+ kitchens.

Harold Becker started Millstone & Co. in 1987 with a single granite chakki in a borrowed garage. He had grown tired of supermarket flour that was roller-milled hot, stripped of its germ and months old by the time it reached the shelf. His neighbours were happy to be the first customers: they brought their own wheat and went home with a bag of atta that smelled like a harvest.

Today Meera Kapoor and the milling team run the same slow process at 21 Granary Lane, Sunridge. We buy Sharbati wheat from 12 partner farms, grind below 40 °C so the natural oils survive, pack the same day and deliver within 24 hours of milling. Nothing is added and nothing is taken out that does not have to be.

Mission & values

Flour you can trace, taste and trust.

Our mission is simple: put honest, freshly milled whole grain on every family's table without shortcuts.

Honest grain

Every lot is tested for moisture, protein and purity. One ingredient on the label, no preservatives, no improvers.

Slow milling

Granite stones turn gently and never overheat, keeping bran, germ and aroma in the flour.

Fair to farmers

We agree prices with our 12 partner farms before sowing, so growers are paid fairly even in a poor season.

Fresh to your door

Milled to order, packed the same day and delivered Tuesday, Thursday or Saturday within 24 hours.

Our history

Thirty-seven years, one stone at a time.

Every milestone started with a customer asking for something the supermarket could not give them: fresher flour, more grains, or a bag at the door.

The first sacks, 1987
  1. 1987

    First chakki opens in a garage

    Harold Becker grinds neighbours' wheat to order, a few sacks a week.

  2. 1998

    Move to Granary Lane

    A proper mill with three granite stones and room for a grain-cleaning line.

  3. 2009

    Millet range launched

    Jowar, bajra and ragi join the wheat, milled in dedicated batches.

  4. 2018

    First home-delivery vans

    Two insulated vans start delivering within 24 hours of milling.

  5. 2021

    Online subscriptions

    Weekly, fortnightly and monthly plans that can be paused any time.

  6. 2026

    12 partner farms, 3,200+ families

    Still family-run, still stone-ground, still milled this morning.
