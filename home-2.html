How it works

Order in two minutes.

Three choices and you are done. Everything after that happens on its own.

Choose your flour

Start with Sharbati whole wheat, then add millets, besan or a multigrain blend. Pick a fine, medium or coarse grind if you like.

Pick a plan

Household, Family or Kitchen. Weekly, fortnightly or monthly. Change it whenever your kitchen changes.

Pick your day & window

Tuesday, Thursday or Saturday, in a Morning, Midday or Evening window that suits you.

Online ordering

Built for people who cook every day.

Your account remembers how you like your flour, so the next order takes seconds.

Reorder in one tap

Repeat your last basket exactly, or edit quantities before the 6 pm cut-off the evening before.

Product swaps

Trade a 5 kg atta for Ragi, Jowar or the Soft Phulka Blend. Household includes 1 free swap; Family, one per delivery.

Lot-code tracing

Enter the lot code on your bag to see the partner farm, milling date and moisture reading.

Saved grind preferences

Fine for phulkas, medium for everyday rotis, coarse for parathas and dalia. Set once, ground that way every time.

Order tracking

Follow each order from milled, to packed, to out for delivery, with a two-hour heads-up from Daniel's team.

Bag-return credits

Hand back your clean paper-lined bags at the next delivery and the credit lands on your next order.

Questions

Ordering and subscriptions, answered.

We mill in small batches and deliver within 24 hours of milling. The milling date and lot code are printed on every bag, and we grind below 40 °C so the flour keeps its natural oils.
