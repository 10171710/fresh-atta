Delivery plans

Fresh flour on a rhythm that fits your kitchen.

Pick how often atta reaches your door. We deliver Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, in three time windows, and every delivery is milled within 24 hours of arriving.

Four cadences

Choose how often.

Every cadence includes free delivery. Switch between them any time, up to 6 pm the evening before your next delivery.

Weekly Fresh

A 5 kg bag every week, milled the day before it arrives.

  • 20 kg a month of whole wheat atta
  • 1 multigrain or millet swap per delivery
  • Priority slots and free bag returns

Best for: families of four or more who cook rotis daily.

Fortnightly Fill

Two 5 kg bags every other week, so the pantry stays full.

  • 10 kg a month, 2 × 5 kg delivered twice monthly
  • 1 free product swap on each order
  • Tue, Thu or Sat delivery slots available weekly

Best for: couples and small households.

Monthly Pantry

A single large drop: atta, millets and besan for the month.

  • Mix any products, from 10 kg up
  • Store sealed in a cool, dry bin for freshness
  • Reminder 3 days before milling

Best for: bulk buyers with plenty of storage space.

Custom Grind

Send us your own grain and we mill it to your texture.

  • Wheat, jowar, bajra, ragi, rice or maize
  • Fine, medium or coarse to suit recipes
  • $0.45 / kg, minimum 5 kg total per order

Best for: farms, cafés and cooks with a favourite grain.

How scheduling works

From your order to your door in 24 hours.

Because we mill to order, your cut-off time is the evening before delivery day.

  1. Day before, 6 pm

    Cut-off. Pause, skip or change your basket until this time. After it, your batch goes on the milling list.

  2. Delivery morning, 5 am

    Meera's team cleans, tempers and stone-grinds your grain below 40 °C.

  3. By 6:30 am

    Flour is cooled, packed in paper-lined bags and labelled with the milling date and lot code.

  4. Your chosen window

    Daniel's drivers deliver in the Morning, Midday or Evening window and send a heads-up two hours before.

  5. After delivery

    Hand back clean bags for a return credit, or rate your batch from your dashboard.

Pause, skip & change

Flexible, with simple rules.

Life changes, and so can your plan. Here is exactly what to expect.

  • Pause, skip or change any delivery up to 6 pm the evening before.
  • Skipped deliveries are never billed, and the plan simply moves on to the next date.
  • Pause for up to 8 weeks at a time from your dashboard. Your slot is held for you.
  • Change day or window for one delivery or all future ones, subject to availability.
  • Swap products within your plan allowance. Extra items are added at shelf price.
  • Cancel any time. No fees and no minimum term on monthly billing.

After the cut-off, your batch is already being milled, so changes are not possible for that delivery.
