Meet the team behind Granary Lane

When people discover Millstone & Co., they often assume we are either a hundred-person corporate food brand or a tiny hobby workshop. In reality, we are five dedicated people working out of a historic brick mill on Granary Lane, grinding grain every morning for more than 3,200 neighborhood households.

Harold Becker Founder & Master Miller 6 Aug 2026 4 min read

The five faces of Granary Lane

Every bag of flour that leaves our doorstep has been touched, checked, and approved by our tight-knit team:

HB

Harold Becker

Founder & Master Miller

Started the mill 39 years ago. Harold still manages stone calibration, granite redressing schedules, and partner farm relationships.

MK

Meera Kapoor

Head Miller

With us since 2009. Meera oversees every morning grinding run, monitors infrared temperature monitors, and tests flour hydration.

NL

Noa Levi

Grain Sourcing & Nutrition

Spends harvest season in Sehore and Maharashtra checking soil microbiology, moisture specs, and fair farmer compensation.

DO

Daniel Okafor

Logistics & Operations

Coordinates our temperature-controlled electric delivery vans so every bag arrives at customer homes within 24 hours of grinding.

We don't want to become a nationwide mega-conglomerate. We want to be your family's trusted neighborhood flour miller for the next forty years.

A morning at the mill

Our day starts at 5:00 am. Harold and Meera check the morning humidity and inspect the grain hoppers. By 5:30 am, the low, soothing hum of the heavy granite stones fills Granary Lane. As fresh flour streams into the cooling bins, the aroma of warm toasted wheat drifts down the street.

By 8:00 am, Daniel has routes mapped and electric vans loaded. By noon, the flour that was a whole wheat kernel at dawn is already in your kitchen, waiting to be kneaded for family dinner.

Come say hello on Granary Lane

Our mill doors are open to visitors Monday through Saturday, from 7:00 am to 7:00 pm. Whether you want to pick up a warm 5 kg bag directly or watch Meera dress the stone furrows, the kettle is always on.
