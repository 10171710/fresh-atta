Billing questions

Good to know before you subscribe.

Still unsure? Write to support@millstone.example or visit the contact page.

You are charged on the same date each month for the deliveries scheduled in that period. Annual plans are charged once up front at the discounted rate. Payments are not processed on this site; no charges are made.

Yes. Cancel from your dashboard up to 6 pm the evening before a delivery and the next one will not ship. Monthly plans stop at the end of the current period, and there is no cancellation fee.

Wheat prices move with the harvest, so we review prices once a year in January. Existing members get 60 days of written notice, and annual plans keep their rate until renewal.

Prices shown are before local sales tax. Any tax that applies to your address appears as a separate line on your invoice. Kitchen plans receive a business invoice with your tax details.

If a bag arrives damaged, stale or not as described, tell us within 48 hours and we will replace it or credit your account in full. Unused annual months are refunded pro rata within 30 days of cancelling.

Pay for twelve months at once and we take 15% off the monthly rate: Household drops from $24 to $20 a month, Family from $44 to $37 and Kitchen from $119 to $101. You can still skip or pause deliveries.
