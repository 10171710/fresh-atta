Company newsFrom the journal

Saturday evening windows are now open

Over the past six months, the most common feedback from our working subscribers has been clear: weekday morning deliveries can be tough to coordinate when leaving for the office. We listened. Starting Saturday, 7 November 2026, we are officially opening our new Saturday Evening (5:00 pm – 8:00 pm) delivery slot.

Daniel Okafor Logistics & Customer Operations 10 Sep 2026 3 min read

Why evening delivery was tricky to engineer

Because Millstone is committed to delivering within 24 hours of grinding, we don't hold standing inventory in a distribution depot. Grains ground at 6:00 am on Saturday morning must be rested, packed into our breathable unbleached paper bags, and loaded onto refrigerated vans by 3:30 pm.

To enable the Saturday evening window, we have adjusted our weekend milling shift. Head Miller Meera and her team now run an early-morning Saturday milling batch dedicated entirely to evening subscriptions.

When fresh warm flour arrives on Saturday evening, Sunday morning parathas and puris become a family ritual.

How to switch your recurring delivery slot

If you already have an active bi-weekly or monthly delivery plan, switching to the new Saturday evening window takes ten seconds:

  1. Log in to your account via the Login Portal.
  2. Go to your Delivery Schedule tab in the dashboard.
  3. Click Edit Delivery Slot and select 'Saturday 5:00 pm – 8:00 pm'.
  4. Confirm changes. Your next delivery on or after 7 November will automatically follow the new time.

Eco-friendly delivery vans

All Saturday evening deliveries will be serviced by our new fleet of 100% electric delivery vans. The cargo areas are kept insulated under 20 °C, ensuring that even during summer heatwaves, your flour arrives cool, fragrant, and ready for your dinner preparations.
