    Flour storage tips

    Keep fresh flour fresh.

    Stone-ground flour keeps its natural oils, which is why it tastes so good and why it needs a little care. Whole wheat atta is best used within 3 months; millet flours within 2.

    • Decant into an airtight steel or glass container and keep it in a cool, dark cupboard.
    • Keep it dry: moisture is the main enemy, so always use a dry spoon.
    • In warm or humid weather, store flour in the fridge to slow the oils going rancid.
    • Never top up an old batch with new flour; finish one bag before opening the next.
    • Smell it: fresh atta smells sweet and nutty, and a sour or musty note means it is past its best.
    • Freeze millet and multigrain flours if you will not use them within 6 weeks.
